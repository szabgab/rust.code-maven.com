## Generic

* [Rust Programmin Language](https://t.me/rustdevs) (English, 1,791 members) - A community for Rust Developers. Follow rules. Don't spam.

## Local and event specific channels

* [Rust Maven](https://t.me/RustMaven) (English). Related to this site and to the [Rust Maven live events](https://live.code-maven.com/rust). See also [Rust Maven on Luma](https://luma.com/rust-maven).
* [Rust in Israel Announcements](https://t.me/RustInIsrael) (Hebrew and English. Announcements only.) Related to [Rust in Israel](https://rust.org.il/).
* [Rust Milan](https://t.me/rust_milan) (English), the Telegram channel of the [Rust Language Milan](https://www.meetup.com/rust-language-milan/) Meetup group.
* [Rust Delhi](https://t.me/RustDelhi) (English), the Telegram channel of the [Rust Delhi](https://www.meetup.com/rustdelhi/) Meetup group. See also the [home page of Rust Delhi](https://rustdelhi.in/)


There are also [WhatsApp](/whatsapp) channels.
